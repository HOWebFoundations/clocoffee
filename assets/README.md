# Assets

Source media for the site. **This is currently the only backup of these files** — they arrived as chat
attachments, and the Higgsfield workspace they came from is on a free plan at 0 credits with unknown
retention. Mirror to R2 before relying on anything here.

```
brand/      identity stills — wordmark, bloom, plain cup mockup, two storefront renders
video/
  source/   the two originals as received, untouched, audio intact
  cups/     per-cup pour clips cut from the reel, cross-fades removed, silent
posters/    first-frame stills for every video surface
```

Colour values and cut points: [`../docs/brand.md`](../docs/brand.md). Every hex there was sampled from
these files — do not re-pick colours by eye from a screenshot, they come out far too saturated.

## Two things to know before building against these

**Both source videos are 576×1024.** Below 720p. That caps the desktop hero until the sources are
upscaled or the pours are reshot.

**The storefront renders are not the shop.** They show two different buildings. Brand presentation only —
they must not appear in the "the shop" section.
